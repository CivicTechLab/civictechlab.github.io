3\. More than Transcendence: Techno-Salvationism and the Reconfiguration of Religious Authority in Asian Buddhism (*TOP STUDENT PAPER*), Religion and Media Interest Group Top Refereed Research Paper Session, Time: Aug 6, 4:45 to 6 p.m. Location: Th087
